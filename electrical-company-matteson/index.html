What to prepare before you call

A few details can make the first conversation more useful. Keep this short checklist handy:

  • The room or part of the home involved.
  • What you were doing when you noticed the problem.
  • Whether it has happened once or more than once.
  • Any change in sound, smell, appearance, or function.
  • Whether anyone has already tried to address it.

If you can do so safely, note which parts of the home seem affected. Don't remove a cover, handle exposed wiring, or keep testing something that appears damaged. Avoid making a repair based on a video or a guess about the cause. These steps can add risk and may make the original issue harder to describe.
